The full journey

The taster is one hour. This is where it deepens.

Over eight sessions, two hours each, you build a real practice of letting words and images meet on paper. Each time you go a little further in, and each time you leave with pages that are your own. Over the weeks they gather into a body of work, and into a way of seeing that stays with you.

  • Eight sessions, two hours each.
  • All your materials, waiting for you.
  • A small, warm circle.
  • Beginning the third week of September.
  • A morning group or an afternoon group, whichever suits your days.

This is for you if

You came to the taster and you are not ready for it to end.

You already create, and you want a steady space that is only for this.

You have been meaning to give yourself the time, and here it is.

When you are ready, here is the full picture: the schedule, everything that is included, and how to join.
